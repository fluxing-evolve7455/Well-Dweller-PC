## 🕹️ About

Well Dweller is a dark fairy-tale metroidvania created by Kyle Thompson, with publishing support from Top Hat Studios and Doyoyo Games. Released on September 22, 2026, it follows Glimmer, a tiny bird armed with a matchstick, as he journeys through a wretched kingdom to burn the wicked queen and save his family. Explore a connected 2D world filled with hostile creatures, strange characters, hidden paths, challenging bosses, and hand-drawn gothic environments. The matchstick serves as Glimmer's light and weapon, making every descent into the darkness feel dangerous and purposeful.

Well Dweller is for players who enjoy atmospheric 2D metroidvanias, dark fairy tales, challenging exploration, and hand-drawn indie adventures.

## ✨ Features

- 🐦 **Glimmer the Bird** — Play as a small but determined hero fighting to save his family.
- 🧗 **Exploration Abilities** — Reach new areas through improved movement and climbable walls.
- 🎨 **Hand-Drawn Style** — Discover a distinctive visual world combining cute characters with gothic fantasy.
- ⚔️ **Boss Battles** — Face powerful enemies guarding the secrets of the decaying kingdom.
- 🔥 **Matchstick Combat** — Use Glimmer's matchstick as both a weapon and a source of light.
- 👑 **Dark Fairy Tale** — Uncover a grim story filled with wicked rulers, witches, scarecrows, and unsettling humor.
- 🏆 **Single-Player Adventure** — Follow a focused solo journey with collectibles, achievements, and story-rich exploration.
- 🗺️ **Connected Kingdom** — Explore an interconnected metroidvania world that opens as Glimmer grows more capable.
